4. Pre-Qualification Documents

Mandatory Uploads: Please upload high-quality PDF/Scanned copies of the following documents. All files are mandatory unless noted as entity-only. Maximum file size per document is 5MB.
Applicant ID Document*

Certified copy of ID Book / Smart Card (both sides) or Passport.

Proof of Residential Address*

Recent municipal account, levy statement, or letter from counselor (not older than 3 months).

CIPC Registration/Compliance*

Valid Cor14.3, CK1/CK2, Trust deed, or Constitution. (Mandatory for non-individuals)

SARS Tax Clearance Certificate*

Valid copy of SARS tax clearance certificate or status PIN code printout.

Company/Entity Profile*

Detailed company profile outlining core service capabilities. (Mandatory for non-individuals)

Contactable References*

List of three (3) contactable references or recommendation letters from previous landlords/projects.

Motivational / Proposal Letter*

Detailed letter/proposal describing the business activity and space layout requirements.

Locality Plan of Property*

Drawing or map showing the physical positioning of the proposed site/space.

Copy of Zoning Certificate*

Zoning certificate copy confirming allowable usage permissions on the site.

Proof of Income / Financials*

3 months recent bank statements, audited financials, or proof of funding confirmation.

Application Fee Proof of Payment*

Electronic Funds Transfer (EFT) receipt or deposit slip for the RED application fee.

By clicking submit, you confirm that all information provided is accurate and all uploaded pre-qualification documents are valid. False or misleading declarations will lead to immediate rejection of the lease application.
